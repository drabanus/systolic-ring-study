CLAIM 3: GPU Approach Suffers from Latency, Not Throughput
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

EVIDENCE:
  • GPU: 500+ GB/s bandwidth, 1000s of cores
  • GPU weakness: Kernel launch overhead ~10 μs per kernel
  • MD needs 5 kernels minimum (distance, force, reduce, velocity, position)
  • Total: 50 μs overhead + 50 μs computation = 100 μs/timestep

  For comparison:
    Serial CPU:  1-10 ms/timestep  (10K× slower)
    GPU:         100 μs/timestep   (1000× slower than ASIC)
    Your ASIC:   1 μs/timestep     (OPTIMAL)

IMPLICATION:
  • GPU good for: Batch processing 1000s of independent simulations
  • GPU bad for:  Interactive simulations, real-time control, adaptation
  • ASIC good for: Everything GPU is bad at
